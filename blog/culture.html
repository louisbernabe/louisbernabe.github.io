Welcome to my blog:

Learning Slavic cultures
and Russian language:

My father was a businessman who travelled frequently, to sell grapevines. He had clients all over the world, in countries such as Brazil, Ethiopia, and of course Europe. As a result of demand, many of his clients were from Eastern Europe, in Ukraine, in Russia, as well as in some ex-USSR countries such as Georgia.

I missed him during his travels. He always brought back interesting goods from his trips. To facilitate his business in those countries, he decided to learn Russian. I was always intrigued by him learning that language, as it seemed difficult and yet had a fascinating beauty to it.

When I was a teen, around 14 years old, he brought me to the same teacher who helped him, to try it out. I only took a couple of classes, to learn the cyrillic alphabet, and a dozen words or so.

It's when I was in business school in ESADE, Barcelona, that my interest picked up. I had several Russian and Ukrainian friends who were very interesting, and I saw the opportunity to learn their language.
