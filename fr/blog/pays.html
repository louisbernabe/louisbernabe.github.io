My life in 6 countries:

I had the chance to live in 6 countries:
France, Spain, US, Russia, Korea and Canada.

I lived 18 years in Montpellier, France before I set foot in Spain for my studies in Business Administration. I was accepted at that time in ESADE, one of the best schools in business, located in Barcelona, Spain.

I then was accepted at Korea Univrsity Business School in South Korea for an exchange while at ESADE. I had the time of my life in Korea, studying hard, having 치맥 (chicken and beer), 소주 (soju, a local rice alcohol), and learning 한글, the local alphabet, as well as partaking in weekly meetups and university studying activities.

During my time at ESADE, I started studying Russian language in autodidact during my junior and senior year. I then spent 3 months in Russia, mainly in St Petersburg, with one week in Moscow, after my senior year.

After getting my diploma in business administration from ESADE, I headed to the US to build a business in Seattle. I built a business in software for 3 years, and while not getting acquired, this was a very valuable experience.

Afterwards, I headed back home to Montpellier, France, where I started a new diploma, in Physics. After a couple of years, my good results allowed me to join University of Toronto as a transfer student. So I spent the next year as a student at UofT (University of Toronto).

I later decided to finish my diploma in France, as UofT was hard on my mental health.
